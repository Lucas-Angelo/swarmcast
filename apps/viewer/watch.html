Connecting… Connecting: loading the stream. Live: you are watching. Buffering: waiting for more video (slow internet, yours or the streamer's). The stream runs 10 to 30 seconds behind the streamer, adjusted to your connection (6 to 10 seconds with "Lower delay"). How far behind the streamer you are watching. It adjusts itself to your connection: it moves closer to live while your internet is steady and further back when it wobbles, so the video does not pause. "LIVE" / "Go live" jumps to the live moment. Settings

While the video fills the screen, the latest chat messages show over its bottom left corner for a few seconds each, above the controls. A small button there opens the message box. Off by default; your browser remembers the choice.
Shares video with other viewers so it runs smoother for everyone. Viewers who also have this on can see your IP address, which only reveals your approximate location (city) and internet provider — not your name, home address or anything on your device. Same as online games and video calls. You can turn it off any time and keep watching normally over HTTPS. Every piece of video is checked, so nobody can send you altered video.

Peers: other viewers you are connected to. The percentage is how much of your video came from them instead of the streamer. Shared: megabytes (MB) you sent to others.
