---
title: Customise your page
description: Put your name, picture, bio, links, colours and a support button or Pix on the page your viewers open. Only people with your link see it.
---

The panel's **Your page** card changes what your viewers see: your name, picture, a short bio, your links and your colours, on the join page and around the video. Only people with your stream link see it; anyone else who finds the address sees the plain "Link not valid" page.

Change what you want, check the **Preview** (watch page, join page and stream ended; computer or phone; light or dark), then click **Save**. Open viewer pages update within a few seconds. The switch at the top of the card turns your page off without losing anything; **Reset to SwarmCast defaults** removes it all.

## Name and picture

- **Channel name:** up to 40 characters. The join page says "Join *your name*'s stream", and it shows at the top of the watch page and in the browser tab.
- **Picture or logo:** a square picture next to your name, also the little icon in the browser tab.
- **Banner:** a wide picture (4 to 1) above the video. It loads after the video starts, so it never delays the live.

Click **Choose a picture…**, drag and zoom to pick the part that shows, then **Use this picture**. PNG, JPEG and WebP work; SVG does not, and an animated picture keeps its first frame.

:::tip[Your photos stay private]
Location, camera and other hidden photo details are removed before anything is saved. Viewers only get the picture itself.
:::

## About you

- **One-line description:** a short line under your name, like "Chill games and chat every Friday". Up to 100 characters.
- **About you:** a few lines about you and your stream, in an "About" box under the video. Up to 1,000 characters and 12 lines. Links typed here are not clickable: use **Links**.
- **Tags:** up to 8 short words about your stream (games, music, cozy…).

## Links and support

- **Links:** up to 6, each with a name and an address: your YouTube, Instagram, a tip page, or an e-mail address. Only `https://` addresses and e-mail are accepted, and viewers see where each link goes. Addresses on your own network, with a password in them or pointing at the stream itself are refused.
- **Support button:** a highlighted button at the top of your page and on the end screen, with the text **Support**, **Tip**, **Donate** or **Pix** (always shown in each viewer's language). It can:
  - open your tip page (**Link to a tip page**: Ko-fi, LivePix, PayPal…); or
  - show your **Pix**: viewers can copy the key, copy the "Pix copia e cola" code, or scan the QR code with their bank app.

For Pix, choose the key type (a **random key** is the best choice: it shows no personal number), type your name as the bank shows it and your city. A CPF, CNPJ or phone key needs an extra tick, because everyone watching will see it.

:::caution[Pix shows who you are]
Anyone who sends you a Pix sees your full legal name and part of your CPF. If you want to stay anonymous, use a tip page instead.
:::

## Colours

Pick **Forest**, **Honey**, **On Air**, **Ocean**, **Clay** or **Graphite**, or **Custom…** for your own colour. It is used for buttons, links and highlights. Viewers keep their own light or dark mode, and SwarmCast adjusts your colour so it stays easy to read in both (the card says when it did). **Corners** sets how round buttons and boxes look: **Round**, **Soft** or **Square**.

## When the stream is shared or ends

- **Show a preview card when the link is shared:** off by default. When it is on, WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord and similar apps show your name, your one-line description and the **Preview card picture** next to the link, to everyone in that conversation. Off, they show a neutral "You're invited to a live stream". After **Generate new link**, the old link's preview stops working too.
- **Message when the stream ends:** shown on the end screen, for example "Thanks! Back on Friday at 8 pm".
- **Show "on SwarmCast" at the bottom of your page:** untick it to hide the line.

:::note
Your page comes from your own computer, through the same live link as the video: nothing is uploaded to another service. Using your own domain name for the link is not supported.
:::
