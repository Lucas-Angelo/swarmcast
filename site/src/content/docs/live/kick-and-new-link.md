---
title: Kick a viewer and make a new link
description: Remove someone from your live, stop someone from sharing video, and replace a leaked link.
---

Every viewer appears in the panel's **Viewers** list with the nickname they typed and their device (💻 PC or 📱 Phone).

## Remove one person: Kick

Click **Kick** next to the person. They are disconnected right away and that browser cannot come back until the live ends.

They could still come back from **another device** if they kept the link. To close that door too, also make a new link (below).

## Stop someone from sharing video: Remove from P2P

Click **Remove from P2P**. The person keeps watching, straight from your computer, but no longer shares video with other viewers. **Allow P2P again** undoes it.

SwarmCast also does this by itself to a viewer whom other viewers report for sending broken video.

## The link leaked: Generate new link

1. Click **Generate new link** in the live link card.
2. Send the new link to your members.

The old link stops working at once for anyone **new**. People already watching keep watching, unless you also click **Disconnect everyone**. After that, everybody needs the new link.

Viewers who try an old link see **Link not valid**:

![The page viewers see with an old or wrong link: Link not valid.](../../../../../docs/img/link-invalid-en.png)

:::tip
Every live gets a new link automatically. A link you shared yesterday does not work today.
:::

## Limits against fake viewers

Two settings below the upload setting stop someone from flooding your live with fake joins:

- **Max viewers at once:** when the live is full, new people see "This stream is full". Nobody already watching is dropped.
- **Sessions per browser:** how many tabs one browser may keep open (the oldest is replaced).

More in [Security and protection](/swarmcast/live/protection/).
