## Who is it for?

For **streamers** who want a live just for their members (subscribers, supporters, a Discord community), next to their public stream on Twitch, YouTube or Kick, or on its own. You keep streaming from OBS as usual.

Your members only need the link and a browser, on a computer or a phone. No app, no account.

## How it works

<HowItWorks lang="en" />

Your computer sends the live; members who watch can pass the video along to each other (P2P), so your internet goes further.

## Free, private and safe

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Free" icon="star">
    No subscription, no ads, no account. SwarmCast is open source (MIT license) and runs on your own computer, so there is no server to pay for.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Private" icon="seti:lock">
    Only people with the secret link can watch, and every live gets a new one. SwarmCast has no telemetry: nothing about you or your viewers is sent to us.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Safe" icon="approve-check">
    Your IP address stays hidden behind an encrypted tunnel, the panel only opens on your computer, and SwarmCast protects your live from fake viewers and attacks.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Simple" icon="rocket">
    Install it like any app. A 5-minute tutorial sets up OBS with you, and updates take one click.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

To be honest about the limits: anyone you send the link to can forward it, viewers who help through P2P see each other's IP address (approximate location only), and the free tunnel has no uptime guarantee. [Security and protection](/swarmcast/live/protection/) explains it all.
