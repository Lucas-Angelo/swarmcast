title: Quick start
description: From download to your first members-only live in six steps, each with its own illustrated page.
---

![The SwarmCast panel during a live, with the live link hidden.](../../../assets/guides/start/en/panel.webp)

1. **Install.** [Windows](/swarmcast/install/windows/), [macOS](/swarmcast/install/macos/) or [Linux](/swarmcast/install/linux/). About two minutes.
2. **First run.** The tutorial checks your PC and walks you through the setup: [First run and the tutorial](/swarmcast/start/first-run/).
3. **OBS, once.** The same scene as on Twitch, a different one, or SwarmCast only: [Set up OBS](/swarmcast/start/obs/).
4. **Go live.** Start streaming in OBS, wait for **OBS: sending**, click **Copy link**: [Go live and share the link](/swarmcast/live/go-live/).
5. **During the live.** [Kick someone or make a new link](/swarmcast/live/kick-and-new-link/), [qualities](/swarmcast/live/quality/), [P2P](/swarmcast/live/p2p/) and [protection](/swarmcast/live/protection/).
6. **At the end.** Stop streaming in OBS, then click **Stop everything** in the panel.

Your members only need the link and a browser: send them to [For viewers](/swarmcast/viewers/). Something wrong? [Troubleshooting](/swarmcast/help/troubleshooting/), and [send your logs](/swarmcast/help/troubleshooting/#send-your-logs) when you ask for help.
