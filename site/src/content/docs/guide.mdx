SwarmCast is for **streamers** who want a live just for their members (subscribers, supporters, a Discord community), next to their public stream on Twitch, YouTube or Kick, or on its own. You keep streaming from OBS as usual.

Your members only need the link and a browser, on a computer or a phone. No app, no account.
